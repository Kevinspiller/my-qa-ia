## Pre-review checklist
- [] Do I have access to every environmnet (dev, staging, production read-only)?
- [] Where are the requirements (Jira, Confluence, README, Figma)?
- [] Who are the test users and roles? Where do I get test data?
- [] Do I have access to the code repository?
- [] Which AI tools are approved here? What am I not allowed to share with them?
- [] Which tools can I connect to the AI (Jira, Slack, Teams, Gmail, meeting transcripts)?
- [] What's the release process, and who decides go / no-go?
- [] Which business rules trip people up? Who can I ask?
